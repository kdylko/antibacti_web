# Google Play Data Safety (AntiBacti)

This document is aligned with the current Privacy Policy page:
`https://<your-vercel-domain>/privacy`

Use these points when filling the Google Play `Data safety` form.

## Data Collected

- Account info (for example: email, user ID).
- App activity and analytics events.
- Purchase/subscription status (store billing records).
- Crash and security diagnostics logs.

## Purposes

- App functionality (account access, learning progress).
- Analytics (app quality, feature improvement).
- Fraud prevention, security, and abuse detection.
- Subscription management and purchase restoration.

## Data Sharing

- Personal data is not sold.
- Limited sharing with processors/service providers only:
  analytics, crash reporting, hosting/infrastructure, and app store billing.

## Security Practices

- Data is encrypted in transit.
- Access controls are used to limit internal access.

## Data Deletion

- Users can request account and data deletion via developer contact details in
  the app store listing.
- Some records may be retained if required by law (for example billing/tax).

## Retention (Must Match Privacy Policy)

- Account data: while account is active + up to 30 days after deletion request.
- Analytics data: up to 24 months.
- Purchase/subscription records: up to 7 years if legally required.
- Security/technical logs: up to 90 days unless needed for investigations.

## Suggested Play Console Consistency Check

- Ensure `Data safety` categories exactly match what is listed in Privacy Policy.
- Ensure purposes in `Data safety` are not broader than policy text.
- Ensure sharing statements in `Data safety` match policy wording.
- Ensure retention in policy is specific (already done) and not generic only.
